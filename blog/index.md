A series on choosing the right kind of AI for a social program's hardest decisions. These posts accompany [AI4SG Finder](https://github.com/ashitpatel/ai4sgfinder), a free, open-source assistant that recommends an AI-for-social-good method for allocating a limited resource, or tells you when a simple rule is enough. Funders can use it to review an NGO's AI proposal, and NGOs to check their own before sending it.

New here? Start with [Part 1]({{ site.baseurl }}/beyond-chatbots/). To try the assistant now, open it in [ChatGPT]({{ site.data.try_links.chatgpt }}), [Claude]({{ site.data.try_links.claude }}) or [Google AI Mode]({{ site.data.try_links.google_ai_mode }}); these links carry the interview and a short funder memo, and the [full setup](https://github.com/ashitpatel/ai4sgfinder#which-way-to-use-it) adds the follow-ups and the funder review.

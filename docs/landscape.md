# Where AI4SG Finder Fits

Several organizations help NGOs and governments use AI for social good. They evaluate AI tools, fund nonprofits to build them, build solutions with partners, or advise funders. This page compares seven of them with AI4SG Finder, using what each one says on its own website.

**In short:** none of the seven publishes a tool for choosing which kind of AI method fits a program's problem. Their closest material does one of three things:

- asks whether AI is needed at all;
- helps choose among large language models (LLMs);
- asks applicants to justify generative AI over other approaches, without saying how to compare them.

AI4SG Finder covers the step before evaluating, funding or building: it helps one program work out what kind of decision it faces and which family of methods fits, including when no model is needed. It complements these organizations rather than competing with them.

## How this was checked

Each claim comes from the organization's own website or a PDF it hosts, read in November 2026. Programs change, so check the linked pages for current details.

Counts by kind of AI follow each organization's own project descriptions. **Named** means the organization states the technique. **Inferred** means our reading of what it describes, for example a "chatbot" counted as generative AI when no model is named.

## What the comparison shows

- **No method-selection tool.** None of the seven helps a program choose between, say, a prediction model, an optimization model, a matching mechanism or a generative assistant for a given decision.
- **Optimization is rare.** Across about 60 listed projects (J-PAL's 16, Wadhwani AI's 24, Fast Forward's 7 AI-powered nonprofits and both Pragati cohorts), only J-PAL's teacher placement project in Rwanda names optimization.
- **Not mostly chatbots.** Generative AI dominates Pragati's cohorts and Google.org's generative AI accelerator, but J-PAL's projects are mixed (5 of 16 generative), Wadhwani AI is led by computer vision (8 of 24), and only 2 of Fast Forward's 7 AI-powered nonprofits are generative.

## How they fit together

A program could use AI4SG Finder to choose an approach, take that brief to a technical partner such as Wadhwani AI, apply for build funding from a program such as Pragati or Fast Forward, and test the result with an evaluation like those J-PAL funds. Dasra and others build the sector's capacity around all of these steps.
